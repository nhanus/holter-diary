The report dialog warns when an entry is still unsaved. **Back to your note**
returns to the draft with its text and time intact. You can save it and export
again to include it, or download a report of the entries already saved.

Starting a diary now confirms that the diary was started. Saving an entry still
shows its own confirmation.

Automated browser tests cover returning to the draft and check the downloaded
PDF before and after saving it. The new flow has not been checked on a physical
phone.
